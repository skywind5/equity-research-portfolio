# Meta Platforms (META) Equity Research Report

## 📌 Investment Thesis
Meta Platforms is a high-margin, cash-generative digital advertising company leveraging AI to improve ad targeting and monetisation.

---

## 🏢 Business Overview
Meta operates leading social media platforms including:
- Facebook
- Instagram
- WhatsApp

Revenue is primarily driven by digital advertising.

---

## 📈 Financial Performance

### Revenue
Meta has demonstrated strong revenue recovery driven by:
- Improved ad targeting
- Reels monetisation

### Profitability
Margins have expanded due to:
- Cost discipline
- Workforce reductions

### Cash Flow
Meta generates substantial free cash flow, enabling:
- Share buybacks
- AI infrastructure investment

---

## 🚀 Catalysts
- AI-driven advertising improvements
- Increased engagement via short-form video
- Continued cost efficiency

---

## ⚠️ Risks
- Reality Labs losses
- Regulatory pressure
- Advertising cyclicality

---

## 💰 Valuation
Using a DCF model:

- FCF: $40B
- Growth: 8%
- Discount rate: 10%
- Terminal growth: 3%

Intrinsic value suggests upside potential depending on market pricing.

---

## 🧾 Conclusion

**Rating: BUY**

Meta remains well-positioned due to:
- Strong competitive moat
- High margins
- AI-driven growth tailwinds
